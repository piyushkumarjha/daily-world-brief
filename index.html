A simple verification habit

Before sharing a story

A quick checklist for a more accurate discussion.

1. Find the original

Look for the official statement, document, court order, data release or direct record behind the claim.

2. Compare reporting

Check whether multiple independent, credible news organisations report the same core facts.

3. Separate fact from claim

Notice what is confirmed, what is alleged, what is analysis and what remains unknown. Check the publication date.
