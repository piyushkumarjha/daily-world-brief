Understand any news in 5 minutes

  1. What happened? State the confirmed event in two lines.
  2. Who is involved? Identify countries, leaders and organisations.
  3. Why did it happen? Look for background, interests and triggers.
  4. Why does it matter? Explain the impact on India and the world.
  5. What could happen next? Note possible outcomes and uncertainties.

For the WhatsApp group

A quick daily routine:

  1. Open the dashboard with morning tea.
  2. Choose one India story and one world story.
